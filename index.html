Lim Soo Peng & Co LLC is a law firm established in 1994 with a focus on dispute resolution through litigation, arbitration and mediation.

Speak to us What we do

Your predicament, recognised and resolved. Legal problems rarely arrive at a convenient time. As a boutique firm, you deal directly with the lawyer handling your matter, who will listen, tell you plainly where you stand and recommend what works for you.

  • Clarity.

    We explain your position and options in plain language, so you know where you stand and what each route involves.

  • Direction.

    We discuss the options available to you and their realistic viability so you can make informed decisions.

  • Peace of mind.

    We listen and help you navigate difficulties, because calm and focus often takes us further.
