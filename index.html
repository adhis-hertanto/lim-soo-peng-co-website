Experience

Your predicament, recognised and resolved. Legal problems rarely arrive at a convenient time. As a small firm, you deal directly with the lawyer handling your matter, who will listen, tell you plainly where you stand and recommend what works for you.

  • Clarity.

    We explain your position and options in plain language, so you know where you stand and what each route involves.

  • Direction.

    We recommend a course of action and tell you why. The decision stays with you, and we make sure you have what you need to make it.

  • Peace of mind.

    We handle your matter with care and keep you informed throughout. You will not be left wondering what happens next.

Litigation

Your case, heard in court.

We represent clients before the Singapore courts, from the first letter of demand through to trial. We prepare every case as if it will be heard, and tell you plainly when settling would serve you better.

Arbitration

A private, binding decision.

We act in arbitration proceedings where parties need a private and binding outcome. We guide you through choosing the forum, preparing the case and presenting it at the hearing.

Mediation

Settle on terms that work.

We help parties reach a negotiated settlement, saving time, cost and relationships where possible. We prepare you for each session, advise on what to accept and help put the agreement in writing.
