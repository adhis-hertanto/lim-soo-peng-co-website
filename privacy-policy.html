4. How we use and disclose your personal data

We collect, use and disclose personal data for purposes that include:

  • responding to your enquiries
  • carrying out conflict checks before we accept a matter
  • providing legal services to you, including representing you in litigation, arbitration or mediation
  • meeting our legal and regulatory obligations, including anti-money laundering and client due diligence requirements that apply to law practices in Singapore
  • managing our practice, including billing, record keeping and administration
  • operating, maintaining and securing this website
  • complying with court orders, legal processes and requests from government or regulatory authorities

5. Consent

We collect, use and disclose your personal data with your consent, or where the PDPA otherwise permits us to do so. By contacting us and providing personal data, you are deemed to consent to us using it for the purposes for which you provided it.

If you give us personal data about another person, please make sure you have their consent, or are otherwise permitted, to share it with us.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer. We will process your request within a reasonable time and tell you of the likely consequences. Withdrawing consent may mean we cannot continue to act for you. Withdrawal does not affect our right to keep and use personal data where required or permitted by law.

6. Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose it, where necessary for the purposes above, to:

  • service providers who support our practice, such as IT, hosting, email, document storage and administrative providers
  • courts, tribunals, arbitral institutions and mediation centres
  • other lawyers, counsel, experts, translators and other professionals involved in your matter
  • opposing parties and their lawyers, where required in the course of your matter
  • regulators, law enforcement and government authorities, where required or permitted by law
  • our professional advisers and insurers

We require our service providers to protect personal data and to use it only for the purposes we specify.

7. Transfers outside Singapore

Some of our service providers, such as our website host and email provider, may store or process data outside Singapore. Where we transfer personal data overseas, we take steps to ensure it receives a standard of protection comparable to that under the PDPA.

10. Access and correction

You may ask us:

  • for access to the personal data we hold about you, and how it has been used or disclosed in the past year
  • to correct any error or omission in your personal data

Please send your request in writing to our Data Protection Officer. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible, and generally within 30 days. If we need more time, we will tell you when we expect to respond. We may charge a reasonable fee for access requests and will tell you the fee before processing your request.

In some cases the PDPA allows or requires us to refuse a request, for example where the data is subject to legal privilege or relates to ongoing proceedings. If so, we will let you know.
